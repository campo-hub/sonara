# AgriStore legal copy — review drafts only

These working drafts are starting points, not legal advice or publish-ready notices. A qualified Kenyan lawyer and the business operator must review and complete them, including business identity, contact details, retention schedules, statutory disclosures, tax/consumer rights, and effective dates.

## Privacy notice draft

AgriStore processes account identifiers and sign-in information supplied through Firebase Authentication; profile, seller, listing, order, review, and report information submitted by users; approximate farm/listing coordinates when a seller chooses to provide them; and technical request information required to secure and operate the service.

We use this information to authenticate users, display marketplace listings, enable buyer-seller transactions, reserve stock, verify hosted payments, manage disputes, prevent abuse, respond to listing reports, and maintain audit records. The API does not receive or store payment card details entered on PesaPal's hosted service.

AgriStore uses Firebase Authentication, MongoDB hosting, Cloudflare R2, Render, GitHub Pages, and PesaPal as service providers where configured. The operator must document each provider's role, processing location, retention, and applicable transfer safeguards before publication.

Account and transaction records may need to be retained for legal, accounting, fraud-prevention, dispute, and security purposes. The operator must set documented retention periods and a user request/contact procedure; this implementation does not provide self-service account deletion or a complete retention scheduler.

## Marketplace terms draft

Sellers are responsible for accurate listings, lawful goods, available stock, safe packaging, and honoring confirmed orders. Buyers are responsible for accurate contact and delivery details and for agreeing pickup/delivery arrangements directly with the seller. AgriStore does not currently arrange delivery or collect delivery fees.

At launch the platform commission is set to 0%. The service currently supports hosted buyer payments to the configured merchant account but does not automate seller payouts, split settlement, escrow, or refunds. The operator must publish how settlement, cancellations, refunds, and disputes are handled before accepting real payments.

Users may report listings they reasonably believe are unsafe, misleading, or prohibited. An authorized administrator may remove a listing and record the action. The operator should publish a review process, appeal/contact path, and expected response times.
